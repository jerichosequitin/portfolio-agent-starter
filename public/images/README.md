# Sample artwork

The four WebP images in this directory were generated with OpenAI Image Generation
for this starter's fictional Alex Morgan portfolio. They are included under this
repository's MIT license and may be replaced or removed.

- `hero-field-notes.webp`: an imagined Pacific Northwest coastline and research-note collage
- `signal-notes.webp`: an illustrative research notebook on a terracotta background
- `civic-index.webp`: an illustrative service directory and map on an olive background
- `open-bench.webp`: an illustrative experiment comparison on a sand background

They do not document real products, people, addresses, clients, research, or outcomes.
Project text and links remain in `content/portfolio.json`.

The interface uses [Cormorant Garamond](https://fontsource.org/fonts/cormorant-garamond),
distributed under the SIL Open Font License through `@fontsource/cormorant-garamond`,
and [Phosphor icons](https://phosphoricons.com/), distributed under the MIT license
through `@phosphor-icons/react`. Their licenses are included in those packages.
