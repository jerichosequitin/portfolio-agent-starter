# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and constructive.
Treat other people with care, assume good faith while evidence supports it, welcome
different levels of experience, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict
participation, or ban a participant when needed to protect the community. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical, with the safety of affected people taking
priority.
